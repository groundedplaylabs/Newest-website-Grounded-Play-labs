Our philosophy

Our mission

Every child deserves to be seen, supported, and celebrated exactly as they are. Grounded Play Labs ABA exists to help children build meaningful, lasting skills — through connection first, always.

Our approach

We practice a modern, compassionate style of ABA grounded in play, connection, and emotional safety. Skills aren't drilled into a child — they grow out of trust and genuinely fun moments spent together. We follow your child's lead, notice what lights them up, and build meaningful growth from there — one building-block skill at a time.

For parents

You are the expert on your child, and we treat you that way. From your very first conversation with us, you're an active partner in the process — never a bystander. We coach you through daily routines and tricky moments, so the progress your child makes with us carries all the way home.

Common questions

Do I need a formal diagnosis before we start?

Not necessarily. Reach out and we'll walk you through what's needed for your specific situation and insurance plan.

What does a typical session look like?

Sessions are built around play, following your child's lead while gently building skills in communication, coping, and independence.

How involved will I be as a parent?

Very involved. You're an active partner throughout, with regular coaching, check-ins, and tools you can use every day, not just during sessions.

How long until we see progress?

Every child's timeline looks different. We track meaningful growth from day one and share updates regularly, so you always know where things stand.

What ages do you work with?

Reach out with your child's age and a bit about your situation, and we'll let you know if we're the right fit or point you toward someone who is.
