Our Core Practice Offerings

Designed to support children, empower parents, and strengthen professional educational teams across California.

01

Staff Training & Behavioral Consultation

Comprehensive consultative support and professional development for schools, clinical agencies, camps, and community care teams. We build clinical capacity, guide tier-1/tier-2 interventions, and train staff in positive behavioral approaches.

  • Educator & paraprofessional workshops
  • RBT supervision & BCBA mentorship
  • Classroom environment optimization
  • De-escalation & proactive coping tools
02

Functional Behavior Assessments (FBAs)

Comprehensive, individualized assessments conducted by Jessica Lewis, MA, BCBA. We look beyond surface behavior to identify the root communication, sensory, and environmental functions—translating findings into clear, respectful action plans.

  • In-depth naturalistic observation
  • Collaborative caregiver & teacher interviews
  • Targeted Behavior Intervention Plans (BIPs)
  • Objective data collection & progress review
03

Parent Coaching & Training

Compassionate, non-judgmental guidance for moms, dads, and caregivers. We coach you through morning routines, bedtime, public outings, and big emotional moments, giving you tools that fit your family’s real-life lifestyle.

  • 1-on-1 collaborative coaching sessions
  • Home routine structuring & visual supports
  • Sibling dynamics & family connection
  • Stress reduction & parent empowerment
04

Social Skills Development

Naturalistic, play-centered peer learning that helps children foster authentic friendships. We guide turn-taking, shared attention, cooperative games, conversational rhythm, and self-advocacy without teaching masking.

  • Play-based peer interactions
  • Perspective-taking & emotional regulation
  • Self-advocacy & expressing boundaries
  • Community and park outing practice
05

California Self-Determination Program (SDP)

Specialized person-centered planning and behavioral services tailored for participants in the California Regional Center Self-Determination Program. We work with your spending plan and independent facilitator to design personalized support.

  • Regional Center SDP participant support
  • Person-centered behavioral goals
  • Community integration & life skills
  • Direct collaboration with facilitators & FMS
06

Individualized Play-Based Learning

Gentle, one-on-one sessions structured around play and genuine curiosity. Every activity builds language, motor skills, functional communication, and cognitive problem-solving in a joyful, supportive environment.

  • Tailored to your child’s unique passions
  • Assent-based, high-trust relationship
  • Continuous milestone tracking
  • Comfortable in-home setting

Meet the Founder

Jessica Lewis, MA, BCBA

Founder & Clinical Director · Board Certified Behavior Analyst

Founded by Jessica Lewis, MA, BCBA, Grounded Play Labs ABA was born from a deep conviction: behavioral science is most powerful when it is rooted in genuine human connection, dignity, and playful joy.

With over a decade of hands-on experience across California public and private schools, clinical centers, family homes, recreational camps, and community settings, Jessica combines evidence-based behavioral strategies with compassionate, developmentally appropriate approaches that foster confidence, independence, meaningful relationships, and lasting progress.
